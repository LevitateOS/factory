# Settings and accounts

Settings control how a project's work runs on this computer. Accounts hold your sign-ins.

## Settings

Open **Settings** at the foot of the sidebar. Its sections include:

- **Execution controller:** whether this computer runs agents for the project, and how many runs may be active at once.
- **Agent roles and judgments:** the model and saved spending source each role uses. Factory never switches a role's source on its own; removing a role withdraws its spending permission.
- **Activity visibility:** which kinds of activity the Work page shows. These are display settings; they do not change how work runs or send notifications.
- **Repository repair funding:** a finite allowance for the Strategist to investigate stalled work and judge repair proposals, set as limits on investigator runs and proposal judgments. Spent charges stay spent; each repair episode allows at most two cycles, and a second needs new evidence.

Changes save per project. When an agent asks for more allowance or access in the feed, its card links to the setting it needs.

## Accounts

Open **Accounts** to manage sign-ins:

- **Coding-agent service:** check for an update to the agent tools and sign in again when needed. The service keeps its own saved sign-in.
- **Git hosting:** add accounts, sign in again when a sign-in expires, or remove an account from Factory. Each account lists the projects that use it. Removing an account keeps your projects and drafts; they reconnect when you sign in again.

Accounts are for sign-ins only. Add, open, reconnect and disconnect projects from Projects.

## Where your credentials live

Sign-ins are saved in your desktop keyring. Agents never receive them directly; the execution boundary acts on their behalf for the actions the project allows.
