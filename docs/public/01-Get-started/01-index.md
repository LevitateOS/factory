# Factory handbook

Factory is a desktop app for Linux. Install it for your own account, pick one of your Git repositories, and steer coding agents from the project's feed while they plan, build, review and merge changes.

## Start here

[Install Factory](02-install.md), then follow [the first-run guide](03-first-run.md) and [add your first project](04-add-a-project.md). The guide takes you from a fresh install to a project feed in four steps.

## How Factory is laid out

The sidebar lists your projects. Open a project to see its pages: Feed, Request, Work, Issues, Pull requests and Knowledge. Settings and Accounts sit at the foot of the sidebar. Press Ctrl K (⌘ K on a Mac keyboard) anywhere to jump to a project, page or post.
