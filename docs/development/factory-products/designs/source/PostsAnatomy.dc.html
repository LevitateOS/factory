Four kinds of card, and how to read the labels

Worker message

Written by an agent: questions, findings, progress, proposals, knowledge.

Factory event

Written by the controller: work started, merged, app ready, allowance holds.

Work record

Recorded facts of a run: reviews, corrections, judgments, checks, merges.

Human post

Yours. JEV replies under it with what it did.
